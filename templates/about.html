ABOUT SIGNBRIDGE

SIGNBRIDGE is an innovative Indian Sign Language (ISL) translation platform designed to bridge the communication gap between spoken language and Indian Sign Language.The system combines speech recognition, text processing, ISL grammar translation, sign recognition, and interactive 3D sign language animations to provide an accessible communication experience.

Features

  • Real-time speech-to-text transcription.
  • Text-to-Indian Sign Language translation.
  • Interactive sign language animations.
  • User-friendly interface.

How It Works

SIGNBRIDGE provides multiple ways to interact with Indian Sign Language.For speech input, the system captures the user's spoken words and converts them into text. The text is then processed into an appropriate ISL structure and displayed through corresponding sign language animations.Users can also enter text directly and receive the corresponding ISL translation and animation. In addition, the sign input feature uses a webcam to recognize hand signs and convert them into readable characters.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build inclusive technology that helps reduce communication barriers between hearing and deaf communities.SIGNBRIDGE aims to make Indian Sign Language more accessible through technology while providing a simple, interactive, and user-friendly communication platform.
