How to use:
1. Click Start Sign Input.
2. Show one ISL alphabet/digit sign to the webcam.
3. Hold the sign steady briefly so the recognizer can stabilize it.
4. The recognized letter is added to the text automatically.
5. Use Space between words.
6. Click Sign with 3D Avatar to send the recognized text to the existing avatar pipeline.
